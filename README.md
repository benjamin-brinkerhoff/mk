# MK: Legacy Arena

An action-packed HTML5 / JavaScript canvas arena brawler inspired by *Mighty Knight* and *Mighty Knight Legacy*. Battle through multiple campaign stages with rich atmospheric landscapes, recruit AI companions from the official roster, unlock and upgrade diverse hero classes, and take down multi-phase bosses!

Live Web Game (GitHub Pages): **[https://benjamin-brinkerhoff.github.io/mk/](https://benjamin-brinkerhoff.github.io/mk/)**

---

## ⚔️ Characters & Party Roster (Faithful to Mighty Knight Legacy)

* **Sir Valen (Knight / Royal Blade)**:
  * Bright royal blue plate armor, blue greathelm pushed up showing messy blond hair and clenched teeth grin.
  * Brown leather belt with gold rectangular buckle.
  * Wields a double-edged pointed broadsword with snappy 3-hit combo slashes (downward chop, upward backhand, and heavy cleave) with glowing crescent blade trails.
  * *Skills*: **Shield Bash** (forward stun charge), **Whirlwind Blade** (360-degree AoE spin), and **Holy Aegis** (invulnerable barrier).
* **Lyra Windrunner (Archer / Green Cap Ranger)**:
  * Green floppy cap draped backward with purple bangs covering her eyes.
  * Green tunic with cross-body leather strap, brown leather bracers, and boots.
  * Wields a carved wooden recurve bow firing **actual cedar arrows with silver arrowheads and red/white feathered fletchings**!
  * *Skills*: **Triple Arrow** (fan volley), **Gale Piercer** (penetrating wind vortex), and **Rain of Arrows** (screen-wide bombardment).
* **Ignis the Sorcerer (Mage / Skull-Cap Pyromancer)**:
  * Deep purple wizard hat with a white skull badge on the band, blue-purple hair, and shouting incantation expression.
  * Purple mantle with golden brooch over dark robes.
  * Wields a tall staff topped with a giant golden/amber swirl lollipop spiral and commands a floating purple grimoire with glowing pages.
  * *Skills*: **Flame Swirl**, **Grimoire Wave**, and **Cataclysm Meteor**.
* **Kael the Shadowblade (Rogue / Red Bandana Assassin)**:
  * Spiky brown hair with a tied red headband, red scarf around neck, and purple tunic with steel kneecaps.
  * Wields dual reverse-grip curved daggers with rapid cross-slashes.
  * *Skills*: **Shadow Lunge** (backstab teleport), **Blade Fan** (360-degree throwing daggers), and **Blade Tempest**.
* **Thoran Ironfist (Berserker / Wild Battleaxe Brawler)**:
  * Wild fiery bushy red hair, bare muscular chest with brown diagonal harness, and spiked bronze shoulder pauldron.
  * Wields a massive two-handed black-bearded battleaxe with realistic directional chops and cleaves.
  * *Skills*: **Ground Breaker** (fissure shockwave), **Berserk Cleave** (bloody axe spin), and **Primal Rage** (double attack speed).

## 🌲 Stages & Dynamic Landscapes

* **Stage 1: Royal Courtyard**: Castle battlements and towers against a twilight sky, waving heraldic banners, flickering sconce wall torches, and cobblestone courtyard paving.
* **Stage 2: Whispering Woods (Mighty Knight Legacy Forest)**: Layered deep midnight-blue/teal forest canopy, tall vertical tree trunks, soft glowing godrays streaming down, lush green grass field, and drifting ambient forest spores.
* **Stage 3: Crypt of the Damned**: Gothic stone arches, tomb pillars, purple soul mist, and cracked ancient flagstones.
* **Stage 4: Molten Depths**: Volcanic caverns with glowing background lava falls, basalt floor, and lava fissures.
* **Stage 5: Frostpeak Bastion**: Snowy pine tree silhouettes, icy mountain peaks, frost veins, and snow flurries.
* **Stage 6: The Dread Citadel**: Shattered floating obsidian pillars, demonic rune circle, and dark void storms.

## 🚀 How to Enable GitHub Pages

To host and play the game directly in your browser:

1. Open this repository on GitHub: [benjamin-brinkerhoff/mk](https://github.com/benjamin-brinkerhoff/mk).
2. Click on **Settings** (top navigation tab).
3. In the left sidebar under *Code and automation*, click **Pages**.
4. Under **Build and deployment** > **Source**, select **Deploy from a branch**.
5. Under **Branch**, select `main` and set the folder to `/ (root)`.
6. Click **Save**.
7. In a minute or two, your game will be live at:
   `https://benjamin-brinkerhoff.github.io/mk/`
